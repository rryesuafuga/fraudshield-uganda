The Fraud Problem No One Talks About

Internal fraud costs Uganda's MFIs and SACCOs billions of shillings every year. Most of it is never caught.

Ghost Loans

Loans issued to fictitious borrowers by staff who pocket the money. Often uses duplicate phone numbers or fake addresses.

Collusion Rings

Loan officers working with borrowers to approve fraudulent loans. Often hidden by geographic clustering and shared guarantors.

Manual Audits Miss It

Traditional audits check only 5-10% of loans. Fraudsters know what auditors look for and adapt their methods.
