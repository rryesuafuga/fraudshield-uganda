The Fraud Problem No One Talks About

Internal fraud costs Uganda's MFIs and SACCOs billions of shillings every year. 69% of Ugandan SACCOs suffer from fraud or governance issues according to the 2021 PROFIRA Report.

Fictitious Borrowers

Loans issued to people who don't exist, with staff pocketing the disbursements. Often uses duplicate phone numbers or fabricated addresses.

Internal Collusion

Staff working with external parties to approve fraudulent applications. Hidden through geographic clustering and complex relationships.

Manual Audits Miss It

Traditional audits check only 5-10% of loans. External audits detect just 3% of fraud cases (ACFE 2024).

What FraudShield Detects

Our algorithms identify six major categories of fraud patterns in microfinance operations.

Ghost Loans

Loans to fictitious borrowers identified by duplicate phone numbers, impossible addresses, and identity patterns.

Rule-Based High Risk

Collusion Rings

Staff and borrowers working together, detected by geographic clustering, shared guarantors, and timing patterns.

Rule-Based High Risk

Unusual Approvals

Officers with abnormally high volumes or approval rates compared to their peers and historical averages.

ML Detection Medium Risk

Loan Stacking

Same borrower with multiple active loans across branches, often with slight name variations.

ML Detection Medium Risk

Timing Anomalies

Transactions recorded after hours, on holidays, or in suspicious patterns that suggest data manipulation.

Rule-Based Monitor

Payment Anomalies

Repayments recorded in the system but money not deposited, or unusual payment patterns suggesting manipulation.
